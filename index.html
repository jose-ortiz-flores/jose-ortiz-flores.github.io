ABOUT

Engineering across air, space, dynamics and control.

My work and interests span spacecraft attitude dynamics, orbital mechanics, atmospheric flight, atmospheric reentry, estimation, guidance and control, and flight simulation. I enjoy translating mathematical models into simulations that can be tested, visualized, and connected to practical aerospace problems.

Alongside engineering work, I teach technical aerospace courses. That experience has strengthened how I structure complex problems, communicate technical ideas, and move between theory and implementation.
